Perform a grammar and academic-language revision of $ARGUMENTS.

Focus only on:
- grammar
- syntax
- punctuation
- articles and prepositions
- awkward or non-native phrasing
- clarity and concision
- academic tone

Do not change:
- scientific meaning
- equations
- mathematical notation
- citations
- numerical values
- technical terminology unless clearly incorrect

Preserve the author's writing style as much as possible.
Avoid AI markers, such as inline m-dashes or semicolons.
Modify only the requested text/section/file.
